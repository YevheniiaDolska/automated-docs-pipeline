Automation Layer To Add First

  • Deploy a pre-commit linter to enforce semantic heading hierarchy and section length limits (target <300 words per section) on all markdown files, addressing the 8.69% chunkability crisis across 1,183 pages and preventing future regressions that degrade RAG and AI answer quality.
  • Implement a CI check that flags any claim-heavy page (>5 assertions) missing evidence patterns (code blocks, image references, log snippets) to systematically close the 58.75% evidence gap (1,041 unsupported claims) and ensure new content includes trust-building proof.
  • Add automated SEO validation (missing meta descriptions, title length, heading structure) to the build pipeline to catch and fix the 25.85% SEO/geo issue rate (336 pages) at authoring time, improving search ranking and reducing manual audits.
