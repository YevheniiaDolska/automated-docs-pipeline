# Full Implementation Services Agreement

This Full Implementation Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}** by and between {{PROVIDER_LEGAL_NAME}} ("Provider") and {{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}} ("Client").

## 1. Scope

1. Provider will implement the full Auto-Doc Pipeline in Client environment(s) defined in Exhibit A.
2. This Agreement includes full docs-ops capability set and knowledge preparation for RAG.
3. This Agreement excludes retrieval-time production RAG runtime unless expressly added by signed addendum.

## 2. Included services

1. Repository onboarding for scope defined in Exhibit A.
2. Pipeline setup, configuration, policy enforcement, and automation wiring.
3. Documentation lifecycle automation (generation, updates, validation, quality controls).
4. Knowledge preparation pipeline:
   - content cleaning, normalization, and structuring,
   - module extraction with metadata,
   - stale-check and contradiction-check,
   - critical conflict exclusion,
   - retrieval index and knowledge graph build,
   - retrieval evaluation gate on test set.
5. Operational reporting and handoff.

## 3. Exclusions

1. Retrieval-time RAG answering in production runtime.
2. Custom premium features listed in Exhibit B unless purchased.
3. 24/7 managed support unless purchased under separate support terms.

## 4. Fees and payment

1. Full Implementation fee: **USD $15,000**.
2. If Client completed and paid Pilot with Provider and is eligible for credit, net Full Implementation fee is **USD $10,000**.
3. Payment schedule: {{PAYMENT_TERMS}}.
4. Taxes excluded.

## 5. Milestones and acceptance

1. Milestones are listed in Exhibit C with target dates.
2. Client has five (5) business days per milestone to confirm acceptance or provide documented material issues.
3. If no response is received within that window, milestone is deemed accepted.

## 6. Change requests

1. Scope changes require written change order signed by both Parties.
2. Change order may revise fees, dates, and resource assumptions.

## 7. Client obligations

1. Provide required access, credentials, and technical contacts.
2. Assign a project owner with decision authority.
3. Ensure legality of all submitted data and content.

## 8. Confidentiality, data handling, and security

1. Mutual confidentiality obligations apply as in pilot template.
2. Provider will implement commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.
3. If required, Parties will execute separate DPA and security addendum.

## 9. IP and license

1. Provider retains pre-existing IP and platform methods.
2. Client owns Client materials and business data.
3. Subject to payment, Client receives internal-use license to deliverables and configured bundle artifacts.

## 10. Warranty disclaimer and liability

1. Services are performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.
2. Except as expressly stated, deliverables are provided "as is."
3. Liability cap and damages exclusions follow the same structure as pilot template, with cap equal to fees paid/payable under this Agreement.

## 11. Term and termination

1. Agreement term begins on Effective Date and ends on completion and acceptance of all milestones, unless earlier terminated.
2. Termination for uncured material breach is allowed with notice and cure period in Exhibit D.

2. Venue: {{VENUE_CITY_AND_STATE_OR_COUNTRY}}.
3. This Agreement may be signed through SignNow; e-signatures are binding.
4. Entire agreement and amendment requirements apply.
