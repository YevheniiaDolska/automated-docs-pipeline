# Pilot Services Agreement

This Pilot Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **{{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}** ("Effective Date") by and between:

- **Provider:** {{PROVIDER_LEGAL_NAME}}, a {{PROVIDER_ENTITY_TYPE}} organized under the laws of {{PROVIDER_JURISDICTION}}, with principal address at {{PROVIDER_ADDRESS}} ("Provider").
- **Client:** {{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}}, a {{CLIENT_ENTITY_TYPE}} organized under the laws of {{CLIENT_JURISDICTION}}, with principal address at {{CLIENT_ADDRESS}} ("Client").

Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## 1. Purpose and pilot term

1. Provider will deliver a time-boxed pilot of the Auto-Doc Pipeline for Client.
2. Pilot term is **twenty-one (21) calendar days** starting on {{PILOT_START_DATE}} and ending on {{PILOT_END_DATE}}, unless terminated earlier under this Agreement.
3. Pilot scope is limited to **one (1) repository** identified as: {{REPOSITORY_IDENTIFIER}}.

## 2. Pilot scope

1. Included:
   - One-repository onboarding (`bundle-only` or `install-local`).
   - Baseline configuration and policy pack setup.
   - Full documentation pipeline capability set for the pilot tier.
   - Unlimited documentation and API-first cycles during the 21-day term within the same repository.
   - Knowledge preparation for RAG (document cleanup, normalization, structuring, module extraction, metadata enrichment, and quality gates before indexing).
   - Weekly automation check and consolidated report review.
   - KPI baseline and handoff session at pilot completion.
2. Excluded from pilot:
   - Multi-repository rollout.
   - Organization-wide change management rollout.
   - Retrieval-time production RAG runtime (unless purchased as add-on or in Full+RAG).
   - 24/7 SLA support.
   - Premium features listed in Exhibit A.

## 3. Deliverables

1. Configured client bundle and runbook artifacts.
2. Reports generated by the pipeline during pilot runs.
3. Handoff summary with findings, KPI baseline, and upgrade recommendations.

## 4. Fees and payment

1. Pilot fee: **USD $5,000** (fixed fee).
2. Payment due: {{PAYMENT_TERMS}}.
3. **Pilot credit policy:** if Client purchases a Full Implementation within {{CREDIT_VALIDITY_DAYS}} days after pilot end, **USD $5,000** paid for the pilot is credited toward the Full Implementation fee.
4. For clarity:
   - Full Implementation list price: USD $15,000.
   - Full Implementation after paid pilot credit: USD $10,000.
   - Full+RAG list price: USD $25,000.
   - Full+RAG after paid pilot credit: USD $20,000.
5. Fees are exclusive of taxes, duties, and withholdings.

## 5. Acceptance

1. Deliverables are accepted when provided and materially conform to Section 2 and Section 3.
2. Client will notify Provider of any material non-conformance within five (5) business days of delivery.
3. Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to remediate confirmed non-conformance during pilot term.

## 6. Change control

1. Any scope expansion beyond Section 2 requires written change approval by both Parties.
2. Scope expansions may affect timeline and fees.

## 7. Client responsibilities

1. Provide timely access to repository, required environments, and decision-makers.
2. Provide lawful rights to all content and systems submitted to the pipeline.
3. Review outputs and provide feedback within agreed response windows.

## 8. Confidentiality

1. Each Party may receive non-public information from the other Party ("Confidential Information").
2. Receiving Party will protect Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and use it only for Agreement purposes.
3. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination, except trade secrets survive as long as protected by law.

## 9. Intellectual property

1. Provider retains all rights in pre-existing materials, methods, templates, and tooling.
2. Client retains rights to Client-provided materials and Client confidential business content.
3. Subject to full payment, Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable internal-use license to pilot deliverables.

## 10. Warranties and disclaimers

1. Provider warrants services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.
2. Except as expressly stated, services and deliverables are provided "as is."
3. Provider does not warrant uninterrupted operation or error-free outputs in all environments.

## 11. Limitation of liability

1. Except for willful misconduct, fraud, confidentiality breach, or unpaid fees, each Party's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid or payable under this Agreement.
2. Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost data.

## 12. Termination

1. Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within ten (10) business days after written notice.
2. Either Party may terminate for convenience with seven (7) calendar days written notice; fees for completed work remain due.

## 13. Governing law and dispute resolution

1. Governing law: {{GOVERNING_LAW_STATE_OR_COUNTRY}}.
2. Venue: {{VENUE_CITY_AND_STATE_OR_COUNTRY}}.
3. Parties will attempt good-faith executive negotiation before filing a claim.

## 14. Electronic signatures

1. The Parties agree to execute this Agreement electronically, including through SignNow or equivalent e-signature platform.
2. Electronic signatures are deemed original signatures and are legally binding to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## 15. Entire agreement

1. This Agreement and its exhibits constitute the entire agreement for the pilot services and supersede prior discussions on this subject.
2. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
